Flowra for Odoo

Demand forecasts, stock-risk signals and purchase orders you approve, inside Odoo.

Odoo 17, 18 and 19 · Community and Enterprise · Free app, LGPL-3

What it does

Flowra reads your sales, purchases, products and stock through Odoo's external API, forecasts demand and flags stock at risk. Whatever it proposes lands in Odoo's own objects: draft purchase orders, activities and internal notes. Nothing is ordered until someone in your team approves it.

One-click connect

An administrator clicks Connect and approves in Flowra. No password is typed or shared: the app creates a dedicated Flowra user and an API key for it, and sends the key to Flowra directly.

Dashboard

Connection and sync status, forecasts, draft purchase orders and open signals, for administrators and purchase managers.

Chat inside Odoo

Ask Flowra about your sales, stock and suppliers from any Odoo screen, as a bubble or a full page. Each person signs in with their own Flowra account; a security group decides who sees it.

Signals as Odoo activities

Flowra's signals arrive as activities of type Flowra on the product, contact or purchase order they concern, assigned to a person in your team, and listed under Flowra → Signals.

Getting started

  1. Install the app (it depends on Purchase, Inventory and Discuss).
  2. Open Flowra → Connection and click Connect.
  3. Sign in to Flowra (or create an account) and approve the connection.
  4. Back in Odoo, the connection shows as active. Flowra runs its first sync, and the dashboard fills in.

Access and data

  • Flowra uses a dedicated internal user, flowra-connector, with read access to sales, purchases, inventory and invoicing, and user rights on purchase and inventory. On Odoo Enterprise it counts as one user.
  • That user cannot edit products or contacts. The app gives it exactly two extra writes, on products, contacts and purchase orders only: schedule a Flowra activity and post an internal note. Activities from Flowra send no email.
  • The app stores no business data of its own. Disconnecting or uninstalling deletes the API key and archives the Flowra user.

Requirements

  • A Flowra account: flowra.lanovatic.com.
  • Odoo 17.0, 18.0 or 19.0, on premise or Odoo.sh, reachable from the internet over https.
  • On Odoo Online, where third-party apps cannot be installed, connect from Flowra directly instead (Flowra → Sources → Odoo).
